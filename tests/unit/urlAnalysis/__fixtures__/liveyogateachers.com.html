Let us help you

We provide yoga teacher recommendations for individuals, companies, co-living spaces, student accommodation and more!

Yoga Teachers for Individuals

Join our online yoga classes, or contact us to organize a private yoga class with one of our teachers. During the week and at weekends for all levels (open level, beginners, and advanced).

Yoga Teachers for corporate yoga

We are a reliable partner for sourcing the best yoga teachers and managing your corporate yoga classes, either in-person or online. We've organised hundreds of corporate yoga and wellness classes, one-offs or recurring, in-person or online.

A large selection of yoga types

We have the largest selection of online yoga classes: Hatha, Vinyasa, Iyengar, Kundalini, Yin, Somatic, Traditional, Pilates, Meditation, Breathwork, and Sound Bath.

Our Yoga Teachers are certified and insured

By booking classes with us you support your local yoga teacher community

Our teachers are based in the UK, US and Europe and teach all types of yoga, as well as Pilates, Sound Bath and Meditation.

Browse our yoga teachers

Certified yoga teachers

Our yoga teachers are accredited after a yoga teacher training course via local governing bodies (like the British Wheel of Yoga in the UK), the Yoga Alliance for Professionals, or yoga associations.

Yoga Teacher Training

Actual training can take from a few weeks (200-hour yoga teacher training), and a few months (500-hour yoga teacher training) to many years (Iyengar yoga training)

Insured yoga teachers

Our teachers have a valid yoga insurance to teach in the UK or in their respective country of residency, and worldwide. Some teachers chose BWY or Yoga Alliance for their insurance in the UK, and others companies like Insure4sport, others well-known global insurers like Hiscox.

Employment status and location

All our yoga teachers are self-employed and can teach online or in-person. Most of them hold many diplomas, and they still take classes with more experienced teachers. In the UK, teachers are based in London, England, Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland and since opening to all markets, teachers have started joining from the rest of the world (France, Germany Switzerland, US...)

Teachers expect you to come so you don't skip the class, and they give you instructions and energy to challenge yourself!

Classes during the day, evening and weekends

Our teachers schedule classes during the week and at weekends for all levels (open level, beginners, and advanced). Let us know if your preferred time and day is missing.

Practise from anywhere in the world

You can practise online no matter where you're based, we offer 6 time zones (in the UK, US and Europe), and we you can pay in 3 currencies: British Pound, US dollar and Euro.
