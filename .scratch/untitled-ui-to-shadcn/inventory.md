**Known limitation:** legacy `styles/theme.css` already claims the specific Tailwind v4 namespaces `--background-color-primary`, `--text-color-primary`, `--border-color-primary`, `--ring-color-primary`, `--outline-color-primary` (and their `secondary` equivalents) for its own "neutral default" meaning, and Tailwind v4 prefers those specific namespaces over the generic `--color-*` one. Verified empirically by building and inspecting compiled CSS (`.bg-primary{background-color:var(--background-color-primary)}`). To avoid silently breaking either system, `styles/shadcn-theme.css` exposes shadcn's `primary`/`secondary` roles under a `shadcn-` prefix (`--color-shadcn-primary`, etc.) instead of the literal `primary`/`secondary` names, and every generated primitive above that referenced `bg-primary`/`text-primary`/`bg-secondary`/`text-secondary-foreground`/etc. was hand-patched to the prefixed name. This is a permanent, mechanical tax on every future `shadcn add` for any component using the `primary`/`secondary` roles — re-check and re-patch on each update per `docs/development-guidelines.md` § Updating generated shadcn source. No other semantic role (`background`, `foreground`, `card`, `popover`, `muted`, `accent`, `destructive`, `border`, `input`, `ring`, `chart-1..5`) collides.
